Bottom line

A credible core, not yet a complete product.

The strongest work is in the DNA virtual machine, canonical scheduler, integrated shell, persistent offline workflows, selected-robot tools, and the Rust-authoritative persistence, ecology, physics, and mode paths. Audited clean-start settings and starter movement now have connected Rust, desktop-domain, Avalonia, CPU, and physical-GPU receipts. The next highest-impact gaps center on robot canonical state, pond and sun ecology, collision breadth, long-tail runtime workflows, shell composition, and original-GUI or historical-corpus proof.

8.94 weighted parity points remain open. That is a feature-gap measure, not a calendar estimate. Late parity work often costs more per point because it crosses simulation, persistence, and interface boundaries.

Exact score: 94.90/100 = 70% × 95.41 mechanics/platform + 30% × 93.70 UI/workflows. The 70% engine share maps to 55% mechanics plus 15% persistence/runtime, audited together.

    How scores are built

    Each feature received an importance weight from 1 to 5 and a parity score from 0 to 100. Domain scores are weighted means. The headline combines mechanics/platform at 70% and UI/workflows at 30%. Online play remains excluded from the rows and denominator.

    The census contains 99 mechanics/platform rows and 78 UI/workflow rows. UI rows also distinguish surface, wired behavior, and rendered fidelity.

    Method: bounded source, serializer, Avalonia-control, and causal runtime evidence at canonical 617eb6d. Focused final gates passed 2/2 Rust defaults, 2/2 untouched starter movement, 3/3 desktop-domain parity, and 6/6 Avalonia workflows. The physical-GPU gate ran on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3050 / Vulkan / DiscreteGpu with CPU fallback disabled. Broad-suite, original-GUI, historical-corpus, and long ecological GPU-soak evidence remain unverified.

    Reading the ledger

    Status definitions

    Parity
    Evidence supports substantially faithful behavior.
    Partial
    Meaningful behavior exists, but important mechanics, workflow, fidelity, or proof remains.
    Stub
    A surface or placeholder exists without required end-to-end behavior.
    Missing
    The required behavior was not found.
    Unknown
    Evidence could not support a reliable determination.

    Evidence limits matter. Catalog registration, synthetic clicks, trace labels, and green unit tests do not independently prove rendered or causal parity. GPU fallback does not prove physical GPU execution.
